Extraction breakdown for the strong-match crawl.

Reproduce with:
  python src/sources/website_vat.py --domains data/results/sample_domains.csv \
      --match-strength strong --out data/results/candidates.csv \
      --cache-only --no-write

--cache-only reads data/raw/pages and never fetches, so the counts are a
property of the captured pages rather than of what the sites serve today.
data/raw/pages is gitignored (36 MB), so a clean clone cannot rerun this
without re-crawling. The numbers below are the record.
